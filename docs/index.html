Hard Hat Detection

Finds construction workers' heads in a photo or video and marks each one as wearing a hard hat or not. The model is a YOLOv8n fine-tuned on two public datasets: 97.1% mAP50 on the Hard Hat Workers test set, 91.0% on a second, more varied one. Code, training and evaluation: github.com/Gauhet/hard-hat-detection.

The model runs in your browser, so your photos and videos are not uploaded anywhere.
